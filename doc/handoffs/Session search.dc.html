Search what was said, from the resume picker

One box, two searches. R stays the resume picker; ctrl+f flips it between the / filter it has today (by title, folder, account or id) and a search of what was said, ranked by match. The two are told apart three ways at once: a mode pair in the heading, a different field (the titles filter is the small / line, what was said is a full-width tinted field), and an empty state that names what it searched. Sample data: query sqlite busy, nine hits across six accounts and five providers, at Tue 13 Oct 2026 14:05, 965 conversations indexed. The table at the foot carries it for the conformance test.

  • Focus. The box opens with the keys in the field, so typing searches. ↓ or ↵ hands them to the results and / takes them back, which is how the board's own / works. Letter keys (H y n a . o) only act once the results have the keys.
